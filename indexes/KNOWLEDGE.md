## Update rule

When adding durable knowledge:
1. Put the note in the narrowest appropriate domain.
2. Prefer one canonical note over duplicates.
3. Link related notes instead of copying them.
4. Add or update index links when a note becomes important enough to discover directly.
5. Do not store secrets or unauthorized confidential material.

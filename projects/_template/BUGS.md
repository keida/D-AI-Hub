# Bugs

Track project defects here.

## Open

### BUG-001 — Short title

- Severity: low | medium | high | critical
- Status: open | investigating | blocked
- First observed: YYYY-MM-DD
- Reproduction: concise, deterministic steps
- Expected: expected behavior
- Actual: observed behavior
- Evidence: logs, screenshots, failing tests, or links

## Resolved

Move a bug here only after verification.

### BUG-XXX — Short title

- Resolved: YYYY-MM-DD
- Cause: root cause

- Verification: exact evidence that the fix works

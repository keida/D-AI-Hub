# Decisions

Record durable product, architecture, or workflow decisions here.

## Decision record format

### YYYY-MM-DD — Decision title

**Context**

What problem or constraint required a decision?

**Options considered**

- Option A
- Option B

**Decision**

State the chosen option clearly.

**Rationale**

Explain why it was chosen.

**Consequences**

List important trade-offs or follow-up effects.

**Revisit trigger**

State the condition that would justify reconsidering this decision.
